# AI Usage

## Tools Used
ChatGPT

## What I Used AI For

I used ChatGPT for brainstorming/refining the story and puzzles, checking the logic of the Toy Derby, generating visual concepts for ASCII conversion, planning the coding flow, and helping me to learn Git/GitHub.

## Incorrect or Unhelpful Suggestions

ChatGPT generated overly complicated artwork that didn’t survive conversion to 80×25 ASCII.
Early toy-riddle approaches that were over done like too much rhyming or rhyming scheme not making sense. 
Various puzzle ideas that were not up to design standards
ChatGPT seems to forget things easily. In the design phase we used a "she" for the possible nemesis but in building it didn't remember that and came up with Dad. Only when I'd prompt it to remember did it get back on track.
It doesn't understand sometimes common knowledge or common sense unless it's reminded that a human probably wouldn't do that.

## How I Verified AI Suggestions
To verify the AI suggestions I reviewed suggestions against my own design requirements.
